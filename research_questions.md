# Five research questions
1. Which approach best correlates with human health meaning-preservation scores?
2. How does the proposed XLM-RoBERTa model compare with existing similarity methods?
3. How does performance vary between English and Hindi?
4. How reliably do approaches detect critical changes such as negation, dosage, frequency, warnings and conditions?
5. What failure patterns appear in difficult health statements and annotator disagreements?

Answer each only after running experiments on a held-out test set. Report real metrics and limitations; do not invent results.
