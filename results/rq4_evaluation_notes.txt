These metrics are NOT human-validated performance and do NOT establish clinical safety.

Limitations:
- Reference annotations are provisional and unvalidated.
- Keyword trigger differences can miss paraphrases and can produce false alarms.
- Actor detection is especially weak because implicit subjects and grammatical roles are difficult to identify with keyword lists.
- This is a baseline for research analysis, not a clinical decision-support system.
- Final evaluation requires independent human labels for each critical-change type.
