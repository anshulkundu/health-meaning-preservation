# Draft human annotation rubric
Score preservation from 1 to 10:
- 9–10: meaning and all critical details preserved
- 7–8: mostly preserved, only minor non-critical difference
- 4–6: partial preservation; a relevant condition/detail changed or omitted
- 2–3: major meaning change
- 1: meaning reversed or a critical instruction/warning contradicted

Inspect negation, dosage, frequency, symptoms, time/thresholds, conditions, exceptions, warnings and care-seeking advice. Suggested labels: 9–10 preserved; 4–8 partially_preserved; 1–3 not_preserved. Have multiple annotators where possible, document disagreements, and get bilingual review for Hindi. This is a proposed rubric and should be finalized before annotation.
